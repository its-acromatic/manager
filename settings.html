Spotlight Guide

Quick Capture: Press Cmd+K or Ctrl+K to open Spotlight from anywhere.

Supported Phrases

  • Dates: today, tomorrow, monday, tuesday, etc.
  • Times: 5pm, 8:30am, 3:15 pm (optional with dates)
  • Types: Automatically inferred from keywords

Examples

  • "physics assignment tomorrow 5pm" → Task on tomorrow @ 5pm
  • "buy stylus nibs" → Reminder (no date needed)
  • "chemistry viva monday" → Exam on Monday
  • "meeting with prof thursday 2pm" → Event on Thursday @ 2pm

Best Practices

  • Write naturally — parsing is forgiving
  • Include keywords: exam, task, meeting, remind, buy, etc.
  • Review parsed data before saving (edit if needed)
  • Dates auto-complete: "tomorrow", weekdays supported

💡 Tip: Spotlight integrates with your tasks, reminders, and events. Quick captures sync instantly to your dashboard.
